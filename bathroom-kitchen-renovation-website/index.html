FROM FIRST CHAT TO FINISHING TOUCH

A clear path forward.

Every renovation is different. This is a typical workflow; exact steps depend on the agreed project scope.

01

Initial inquiry

Share the room, your ideas and what you want to change.

02

Project discussion

Talk through priorities, style, practical needs and scope.

03

Site visit, if needed

Discuss measurements and existing conditions if appropriate.

04

Scope & quotation

Clarify proposed work, materials, assumptions and costs.

05

Plan & schedule

Agree next steps and timing before work is arranged.

06

Renovation work

Carry out the agreed work and coordinate confirmed trades.

07

Final review

Review the completed scope and any follow-up items.
