BLUSH & BLOOM BEAUTY STUDIO — WEBSITE PROJECT

FILES
- index.html: complete single-page website
- style.css: responsive styling
- script.js: mobile navigation, service selection, gallery viewer and WhatsApp booking form
- assets/: locally bundled original SVG illustrations

HOW TO RUN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser, or upload the whole folder to your web host.
3. Keep the assets folder beside index.html. Do not move the files independently.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Replace the demo brand name, descriptions and illustrations with the real business identity and approved photography.
- Verify that WhatsApp number +923266438812 is the correct business number.
- Add confirmed address, opening hours, email, social links, service list and prices.
- Booking requests open WhatsApp; this site has no server/database and does not automatically confirm appointments.
- The bundled illustrations are original SVG artwork, not photographs of actual clients or salon work.
- Google Fonts requires an internet connection; system fallbacks are included.
