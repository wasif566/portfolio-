Questions, answered.

Still unsure what your item needs? Send a few details and ask us directly.

Ask about your item ↗
How long does carpet cleaning take?+

Timing depends on the number of rooms, their size, condition, access and method selected. Share these details for a more useful estimate.

How long will carpets take to dry?+

Drying time varies with fibre, moisture level, airflow, temperature and the cleaning method. Follow the care advice provided for your specific item.

Can you remove old stains?+

Some stains may improve, but removal cannot be guaranteed. Age, dye, fibre and previous cleaning attempts can affect the result.

Do you clean sofas and mattresses?+

Use this website to enquire about sofa, upholstery and mattress care. Confirm which services are currently offered before booking.

Do I need to move furniture?+

Furniture handling depends on the item, access and agreed service. Please mention heavy or delicate furniture when requesting your quote.

Are cleaning products included?+

Ask for the quote to clarify what products, materials and treatments are included for your selected service.
