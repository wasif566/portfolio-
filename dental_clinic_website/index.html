HAVE QUESTIONS?

Helpful answers, before you visit

These general answers should be reviewed and approved by your clinic before publishing.

Still have a question? Contact us ↗
How do I request an appointment?+

Complete the appointment request form. It opens WhatsApp with your details so you can send them to the clinic. Wait for the clinic to respond before treating the appointment as confirmed.

Are all listed treatments available?+

Service availability can vary. Please contact the clinic to confirm which treatments are offered before booking.

Can I ask about fees before booking?+

Yes. Contact the clinic to ask about consultation fees, treatment costs, and any other charges relevant to your visit.

What if I need urgent dental help?+

Contact the clinic directly to ask about urgent availability. If you have a serious or rapidly worsening medical emergency, seek urgent local medical care.
