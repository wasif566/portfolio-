Before you visit

Your patient guide

A few simple steps can make arranging a test easier. Contact the laboratory if anything is unclear.

01

Book or enquire

Share the requested test or service, your preferred branch and a suitable time. The laboratory will confirm availability and whether an appointment is needed.

02

Check preparation

Preparation differs by test. Follow instructions from your healthcare professional or laboratory; do not stop medication or change treatment based on generic website text.

03

Bring the details

Bring any test request or referral you were given and the information the laboratory says is required. Ask ahead if you are unsure what to bring.

04

Ask about reports

Confirm expected timing and the approved report collection method with the laboratory. Never share passwords or sensitive report details in a public enquiry.

i

Important: Always follow the instructions supplied by your healthcare professional or laboratory. Website information is general guidance and does not replace professional medical advice.

Need to know

Frequently asked questions

These are general answers for the website starter. Customize them to match your laboratory's actual process.

Still have a question?
Do I need an appointment?

Appointment requirements depend on the test and branch. Contact the laboratory to confirm before visiting.

Which tests are available?

Browse the test catalogue for examples, then confirm current availability with the laboratory. Replace example entries with the verified service list before launch.

Do I need to fast before a test?

Not every test requires fasting. Follow instructions from your healthcare professional or laboratory, and ask them if you are uncertain.

Is home sample collection available?

Availability, eligible areas, time windows and fees must be confirmed directly with the laboratory.

How can I receive my report?

Ask the laboratory about its approved report collection procedure. Do not send passwords or sensitive report details through an ordinary website form.

How long do reports usually take?

Timing depends on the specific test and laboratory process. Contact the laboratory for an estimate; no turnaround time is guaranteed by this page.

How can I confirm test prices?

Use the appointment enquiry form or call the laboratory. Add prices only after they have been verified and approved for publication.

What if I have a question about my report?

Contact the laboratory through its official channel and speak with your treating healthcare professional about clinical interpretation.
