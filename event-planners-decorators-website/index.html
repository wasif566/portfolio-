Questions, answered

A few things to know first.

Every event is different. If you don’t see your question here, send us an enquiry and we’ll discuss your requirements.

Contact details ↗
How far in advance should I book?+

Lead times depend on event size, date, venue, and service scope. Share your date as early as you can so the team can discuss availability. No date is held until confirmed directly.

Can I request a custom theme?+

Custom themes can be discussed as part of your enquiry. Share inspiration, colours, and details that matter to you so the proposed scope can be tailored.

Do you provide décor rentals?+

Rental availability depends on the business’s actual inventory and supplier arrangements. Confirm which items are offered before publishing this FAQ as a promise.

Do you work at external venues?+

Venue coverage and access rules vary. Share the venue name or location and the team can discuss feasibility, permissions, and any venue requirements.

Can you work within my budget?+

Share your estimated budget and priorities. The team can discuss possible options, but the final scope depends on the event requirements and agreed costs.

What is included in the packages?+

Package cards on this demo are examples. Your actual proposal should list the confirmed services, exclusions, setup and breakdown responsibilities, and any optional extras.

Do you manage vendors?+

Vendor coordination should only be offered if it is part of your actual service. Confirm which suppliers are managed by your team and which are booked separately by the client.

What are your payment and cancellation policies?+

Payment milestones, deposits, changes, and cancellations must follow your written business terms. Replace this answer with your current policy before publishing.
