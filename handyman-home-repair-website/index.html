GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Not sure if your job is a fit? Send a short description and we’ll help clarify the next step.

Ask us about your repair →
What repairs do you handle?+

Typical tasks can include furniture assembly, shelf installation, minor wall repairs, door hardware adjustments, cabinet fixes and general upkeep. We confirm each job individually.

Do you charge a call-out fee?+

Call-out, travel and minimum labour charges must be confirmed by the business before booking. Ask for the full expected cost when requesting a quote.

Can I book a specific time?+

You can share your preferred date and time. The visit is only confirmed once the business replies and agrees availability with you.

Do you supply materials?+

Materials and parts depend on the task. Tell us what you already have, and agree who will source items and how they will be charged before work starts.

Do you offer recurring property maintenance?+

Recurring maintenance can be discussed for suitable properties. Frequency, scope, access, response times and pricing need to be agreed in advance.

Which areas do you cover?+

Service areas are configurable and must be completed with the business’s actual coverage. We’ll confirm your location and any travel charges before arranging a visit.

Do you work with landlords?+

Landlord and rental-property requests can be discussed, including maintenance lists and access arrangements. Specialist compliance work may need separate contractors.

Which jobs require specialist contractors?+

Electrical, gas, structural, major plumbing, hazardous-material and other regulated or specialist work may require appropriately qualified professionals. We do not claim to provide these services unless verified qualifications and permissions are in place.
