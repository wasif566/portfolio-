Good to know

Questions before moving day?

Every move is different. If your question isn't covered here, send an enquiry and include the details that matter to you.

Ask us directly ↗
?
How far in advance should I book?

As early as practical, especially for month-end, weekends or busy seasons. Your date is not confirmed until availability and booking terms have been agreed.

Do you provide packing materials?

Packing materials and packing assistance should be confirmed when you request a quote. Tell us whether you need boxes, wrapping or help packing.

Can you move fragile items?

List fragile, valuable, oversized or unusually shaped items in advance. The team must confirm suitable handling and any service limitations before booking.

Do you disassemble furniture?

Furniture disassembly and reassembly may depend on the item and crew availability. Include photos or descriptions in your enquiry so it can be assessed.

How are moving costs calculated?

Quotes commonly consider the inventory, distance, access and floor levels, packing or handling needs, timing and any special requirements. Request a tailored quote for your move.

Do you offer office relocation?

Office relocation enquiries are welcome. Share the number of workstations, equipment, access restrictions and preferred schedule so availability can be confirmed.

Are long-distance moves available?

Longer-distance moves are subject to route, date and service availability. Provide both locations to confirm whether the journey can be arranged.

What happens if my date changes?

Contact the moving team as soon as possible. Changes depend on availability and the agreed booking terms; do not assume a new date is reserved until confirmed.
