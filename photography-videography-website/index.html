A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Questions, answered.

Every project has its own details. If something isn't covered here, just ask.

Ask us directly ↗
How far in advance should I book?+

Popular dates can book early, especially for weddings and major events. Share your date as soon as possible; availability is only confirmed by the studio.

Do you provide both photography and videography?+

Photo and film options may be available depending on the project, date and crew. Tell us what you need and we'll confirm options in your quote.

How will edited files be delivered?+

Delivery method, format, access period and timeline will be agreed in writing before booking.

Can I request a custom package?+

Yes—outline your coverage, deliverables and budget in the enquiry form. We'll confirm whether a tailored option is possible.

Are travel charges applicable?+

Travel or accommodation costs may apply depending on location. These will be discussed in your quote where relevant.

Do you provide printed albums?+

Album options and print products can be discussed as part of your enquiry, with costs confirmed in the quote.

Can I request additional editing?+

Additional retouching or revisions may be available. Scope, turnaround and any extra fee will be agreed before work begins.

What is the cancellation policy?+

The signed agreement will set out cancellation, rescheduling, retainer and refund terms. Review those terms before confirming.
