BEFORE YOU GO

A few notes for the road.

A little preparation can make the journey smoother. Always verify destination-specific rules close to departure.

01 / DOCUMENTS

Check before you pack

Review passport validity, visa requirements, transit rules and any documents required for your specific itinerary.

02 / SMART PACKING

Pack for your plans

Check seasonal conditions and planned activities. Keep essential medication and important documents accessible.

03 / PEACE OF MIND

Leave room for the unexpected

Consider travel insurance, keep emergency contacts handy and allow a sensible buffer for connections.

ⓘ Entry requirements and travel advisories change. Confirm the latest information with official government sources, embassies and relevant transport providers before departure.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you set off.

Here are a few helpful starting points. We can clarify the details for your particular trip when you enquire.

Still curious? Ask us →
How do I request a travel quotation?+

Complete the enquiry form with your destination, dates, group size and preferences. We can then discuss suitable options. Submitting a form is not a booking.

Are flights included in package prices?+

Not automatically. Flights and other inclusions depend on the final written quote. Ask us to specify what is and isn't included before confirming.

Can I customize a holiday package?+

Yes, sample concepts are a starting point. Share your preferred pace, activities, accommodation style and budget so the plan can be tailored.

Do you assist with visa applications?+

Visa information support may be available depending on the agency's actual services. Eligibility and decisions are determined by the relevant authorities.

Are hotel stays included?+

Accommodation is only included when explicitly listed in your confirmed itinerary or quotation.

What are the payment and cancellation policies?+

These depend on the specific suppliers and services. Ask for the applicable payment schedule, cancellation terms and refund conditions in writing before paying.

Are package prices guaranteed?+

No sample price is shown here. A quote should state its validity period; supplier prices and availability can change until confirmed.

What documents should I check before travelling?+

Check passport validity, visas, transit requirements, tickets, accommodation details and any health or entry requirements through official sources.
